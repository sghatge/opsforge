# Process SOP Template

Use this structure when documenting a process manually (the SOP generator
produces a draft in the same shape).

```markdown
# Standard Operating Procedure — <Process Name>

## 1. Process
Name, owner, short description.

## 2. Purpose
Why the process exists and what "done well" means.

## 3. Scope
Where the SOP applies; what is explicitly out of scope.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |

## 5. Procedure
Numbered steps in execution order. One action per step.

## 6. Acceptance criteria
Objective, checkable conditions for a valid outcome.

## 7. Escalation
What to do when a step cannot be completed as documented.

## 8. Review cycle
Review frequency and trigger events (tooling or policy changes).
```

Automate-first candidates: steps that are repeatable, rule-based and
evidence-producing. Keep human judgement on: exceptions, approvals,
customer-sensitive communication.
